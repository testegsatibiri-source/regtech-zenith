## Values transcribed into the pack

`PH_PARAMS.sss.table` — 61-step table, MSC ₱5,000–₱35,000: employee share 5%
of MSC, employer share 10% of MSC, EC ₱10 (MSC ≤ ₱14,500) / ₱30 (MSC ≥
₱15,000). Rows are generated deterministically from the published rate
schedule (₱500 compensation brackets; bracket ₱m−250 to ₱m+249.99 maps to MSC
₱m). Quick bounds: `mscMin: 5_000`, `mscMax: 35_000`.

The MSC slice above ₱20,000 funds the Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF / MySSS
Pension Booster). The pack's BenefitsOutput surfaces EE/ER **totals** only, so
the Regular-SS-vs-MPF split is not itemized; totals are unaffected (5%/10% of
the full MSC either way). Itemizing MPF is a possible future enhancement and
does not block correctness of remitted amounts.
