## Cited source

- Republic Act No. 11199 (Social Security Act of 2018) — graduated contribution
  schedule, Section 4(a)(9).
- SSS Circular 2024-004 — 2024 Schedule of Contributions (14% total: 4.5%
  employee / 9.5% employer, EC ₱10 below MSC ₱15,000 and ₱30 at or above,
  MSC range ₱4,000–₱30,000), effective 2024-01-01.
- Published: Official Gazette / sss.gov.ph circulars portal.

## Values transcribed into the pack

`PH_PARAMS.sss.table` — 51-step table: MSC ₱4,000–₱30,000, employee share 4.5%
of MSC, employer share 9.5% of MSC, EC ₱10 (MSC < ₱15,000) / ₱30 (MSC ≥ ₱15,000).
Quick bounds: `mscMin: 4_000`, `mscMax: 30_000`.

## Reconciliation result (2026-09-16)

**STALE.** SSS Circular 2024-006 (SSC Resolution 560-s.2024), effective
2025-01-01, raised the combined rate to 15% (5% EE / 10% ER) and the MSC range
to ₱5,000–₱35,000, adding a Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF/WISP) tier above the
regular MSC. The pack table is the 2024 schedule and no longer matches the
values in force.

Values are intentionally **unchanged** in H24 (zero-calculated-value-change
sprint). Correction requires a params/rulesetVersion bump, re-signature and
golden re-baseline — tracked as **DEBT-030**.
